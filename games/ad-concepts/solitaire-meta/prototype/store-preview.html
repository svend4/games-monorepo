Description

Come home to Orchard Peaks — a warm, unhurried solitaire where every hand you play brings a neglected family orchard back to life.

You've inherited Aunt Martha's old orchard on the hills, somewhere between Vermont and the Cotswolds: a greenhouse, a pond, a beehive, and a very good dog named Biscuit who keeps stealing the gardening gloves. Clear the cards, gather the harvest, and fulfil gentle garden orders. Season by season, the garden grows and tells its quiet story.

  • No timers, no move limits, no penalties for pausing — take your time.
  • Extra-large cards and buttons, high-contrast text, one-tap “Large text” preset.
  • Simple TriPeaks rule: remove a card one rank above or below the pile; Ace and King are neighbours.
  • A garden with a story: twelve watercolour zones, warm little scenes, a herbarium to collect.
  • Fair & honest: rewarded ads are always optional; nothing you buy purchases a win.

No casino glitz. No pressure. Just a beautiful garden, a calm deck of cards, and all the time in the world. Pour yourself a cup of tea and start restoring Orchard Peaks today.
